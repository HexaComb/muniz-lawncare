# Design

Landscape editorial: real work photographed generously, warm paper, forest green, understated golden accents inherited from the original logo. Large Manrope display lettering and DM Sans reading text. Use the original business logo and real business imagery. Lawn accents and equipment illustrations add character without inventing brand marks or proof.

The homepage is a Persuade surface. The opening is an immersive video canvas with left-aligned bold sales copy; the next section is deliberately visible at the fold. Services use two substantial photographic panels followed by concise supporting services. The owner story pairs a candid crew portrait with spacious text. Process uses a property-plan illustration, mower and lawn chair instead of numbered steps. A comparison swiper uses explicitly labeled sample photos pending a real before/after pair. Local coverage is expressed honestly as Patterson and surrounding communities. Verified Google review excerpts precede a clear estimate form.

Motion: mower passes reveal the heading and subtitle simultaneously in opposite directions, at 1.8 seconds per line; the background settles. A floating ringing phone icon links to the contact form; Buttons and links have quiet directional feedback. Reduced-motion visitors get a poster and no entrance animation. Video pauses when out of view and can be paused manually.

Local review only. No infrastructure provisioning, hosted CI configuration or deployments without an explicit user request. Form submission is a clearly labeled local preview pending the owner's preferred integration; existing Jobber booking is linked independently.
