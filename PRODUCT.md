## Platform

## Stack
User selected TanStack and Cloudflare hosting with previews enabled. Implement with TanStack Start and the Cloudflare Vite plugin.

## Users
Local homeowners seeking recurring lawn care or yard transformations, with equal emphasis on both.

## Product Purpose
An image-driven marketing website for Muniz Lawn Care & Maintenance LLC that turns visitors into estimate inquiries.

## Brand Commitments
Real business photography and preferably an Instagram video hero with bold sales copy and meaningful entrance motion. Section order: hero, services, business background, process, service areas, Google reviews, contact form.

## Evidence on Hand
Verified first-party business history, services and phone are recorded in research/brief.md. Optimized website photography and logo are in public/images; provenance is in research/media-sources.json. Based in Patterson, established in 2008, owned by Rodrigo Muniz.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Do not invent testimonials, ratings, service cities, response guarantees or a form destination. Full email address remains unverified. Google rating and excerpts were checked October 30, 2026. No live-domain replacement requested. Working website first selected by user.

## Deployment boundary
Work locally first. The user will connect GitHub and Cloudflare CI when satisfied with the design. Provisioning resources, configuring hosted CI and deploying require a specific user request. Personal Cloudflare account is the eventual destination; no account ID is needed in the local config.
